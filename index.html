About

Hi, I'm Marcel.

Since founding Marcel Studios™ in 2015 as a modest design studio, I've grown it into a full-service agency driven by world-class creatives and producers.

My name is an extension of my design philosophy—create meaningful solutions for the client/user and not for ourselves. I begin every project with an open mind, curious spirit, and without preconceived ideas.

Based between Accra, Ghana and Davis, California, I work across time zones to help brands bring their vision to life. Whether it's brand strategy, digital products, or visual design—I believe great design is how it works, not just how it looks.
